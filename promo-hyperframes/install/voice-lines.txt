One file per line, named 1.mp3 ... 12.mp3. The video will be re-timed to your recordings.

1. Installing NexDB? You might see a warning. Here's why.
2. NexDB isn't code-signed yet. Those certificates cost money every year, and we're a small team.
3. So Windows and macOS just don't know us yet. NexDB itself is safe to install.
4. On Windows, open the installer. You'll see this blue screen.
5. Click "More info". Then click "Run anyway".
6. Done. It installs like any other app.
7. On Mac, drag NexDB to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS blocks it.
8. Click "Done". Not "Move to Trash".
9. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
10. Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.
11. Done! You only do this once.
12. Only download NexDB from db dot nexiodev dot site.
